# COUP — Play Reference Card

## Turn rule

Take **one Action** on your turn.

- If you begin your turn with **10 or more coins**, you **must launch a Coup**.

## General actions

These actions do **not** require claiming a character.

### Income
- **Action:** Income
- **Effect:** Take **1 coin**.
- **Challenge:** Cannot be challenged.
- **Block / counteraction:** Cannot be blocked.

### Foreign Aid
- **Action:** Foreign Aid
- **Effect:** Take **2 coins**.
- **Challenge:** Cannot be challenged.
- **Block / counteraction:** Can be blocked by a player claiming **Duke**.

### Coup
- **Action:** Coup
- **Cost:** Pay **7 coins**.
- **Effect:** Choose another player; that player **loses 1 influence**.
- **Challenge:** Cannot be challenged.
- **Block / counteraction:** Cannot be blocked.

## Character actions and counteractions

Character actions require the acting player to claim the relevant character. Such claims may be challenged under the normal challenge rules.

### Duke
- **Action:** Tax
- **Effect:** Take **3 coins**.
- **Can be blocked:** No.
- **Counteraction:** **Blocks Foreign Aid.**

### Assassin
- **Action:** Assassinate
- **Cost:** Pay **3 coins**.
- **Effect:** Choose another player; that player **loses 1 influence** if the assassination succeeds.
- **Can be blocked:** Yes. Only the player being assassinated may claim **Contessa** to block it.
- **Counteraction:** None.

### Ambassador
- **Action:** Exchange
- **Effect:** Exchange cards with the **Court Deck**.
- **Detailed effect:** Draw 2 cards from the Court Deck, choose which cards to keep among those and your current face-down influence cards, then return 2 cards to the Court Deck.
- **Can be blocked:** No.
- **Counteraction:** **Blocks stealing.**

### Captain
- **Action:** Steal
- **Effect:** Take **2 coins from another player**. If that player has only 1 coin, take only 1.
- **Can be blocked:** Yes, by a player claiming **Captain** or **Ambassador**.
- **Counteraction:** **Blocks stealing.**

### Contessa
- **Action:** None.
- **Effect:** None.
- **Counteraction:** **Blocks assassination.**

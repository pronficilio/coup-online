# COUP Summary Card

**Income** Take 1 coin.  
*Cannot be blocked or challenged.*

**Foreign Aid** Take 2 coins. *Cannot be challenged. Can be blocked by player claiming Duke.*

## DUKE

**Tax** Take 3 coins. *Cannot be blocked.*

## CAPTAIN

**Steal** Take 2 coins from another player.  
*Can be blocked by Captain or Ambassador.*

## ASSASSIN

**Assassinate** Pay 3 coins, choose player to lose influence. *Can be blocked by Contessa.*

## CONTESSA

Block assassination. *Cannot be blocked.*

## AMBASSADOR

**Exchange** Take 2 cards, return 2 cards to Court deck. *Cannot be blocked.*

**Coup** Pay 7 coins, choose player to lose influence. *Cannot be blocked or challenged.*

**If 10+ coins, must Coup**
